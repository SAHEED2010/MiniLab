# CAD and enclosure

**Status: Planned; no enclosure exists yet.**

The enclosure will eventually protect the electronics while keeping the first CAD exercise simple enough to understand and revise. Dimensions should be based on the assembled prototype rather than guesses made before the hardware is selected.

## Enclosure goals

- Protect the electronics during ordinary handling.
- Expose the OLED display clearly.
- Provide access to the button or other controls.
- Provide safe access for USB or the selected power connection.
- Expose sensors appropriately so the enclosure does not distort measurements unnecessarily.
- Remain simple enough for a first CAD project.

## Future design notes

The CAD process should record the selected material, mounting assumptions, clearances, access openings, and revisions. Candidate approaches could include a simple 3D-printed enclosure, laser-cut panels, or an early cardboard/prototyping mock-up; no approach has been selected yet.
